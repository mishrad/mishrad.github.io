Selected work

Demand modeling for pricing decisions

Amazon Retail Pricing · Economist Intern

How should a retailer evaluate discounts and bundle prices? I combined product-text embeddings and gradient boosting with demand modeling, reducing forecast RMSE by 5% relative to standard logit models. I presented pricing recommendations to inform future A/B tests.

Measuring policy effects and informing allocation

Pennies for Pads · With Ritika Sethi · Under review

Do subsidized menstrual products increase adoption? We use doubly robust difference-in-differences to estimate a 9.6 percentage-point increase in pad use. The interactive pharmacy planner illustrates efficiency and equity tradeoffs in choosing locations.

Understanding and evaluating AI decisions

Decodable but Unused · Accepted at ALTA 2026

Can a model encode the better option without choosing it? I use hidden-state probes and activation tests to study the gap between information represented inside language models and their choices.

In a separate Apart Research sprint report on agent collusion, I contributed to experiment design, Python labeling using Modal, and writing.
