About

I am an economist with a PhD in Economics from Rice University and an MS in Computer Science from Georgia Tech. My work combines industrial organization, econometrics, causal inference, and machine learning to study hospital choice, pricing, and the economics of AI.

I am currently a fellow in the Supervised Program for Alignment Research (SPAR), estimating demand for AI models and willingness to pay for capability and safety under the mentorship of Pavel Kocourek.

Previously, I was an Associate at Cornerstone Research (January 2025–September 2026), applying demand models and causal methods to competition and litigation questions. At Amazon Retail Pricing (May–August 2023), I combined product-text embeddings and gradient boosting to reduce demand forecast RMSE by 5% relative to standard logit models.

Earlier roles include research at the Indian School of Business and Business and Regulatory Risk Research at HSBC. I work primarily in Python, SQL, R, and Stata; my resume covers professional experience and technical skills, and my CV includes teaching, awards, and service.
