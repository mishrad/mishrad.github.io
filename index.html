About

I am an economist and engineer. I hold a PhD. in Economics from Rice University and an MS in CS from Georgia Tech. My research interests lie in Industrial Organization, Causal Inference, and AI. My current research explores data intensive projects on healthcare and market design focusing on India. Recently, I have also been dablling in interpretability of large language models and how they can be used with economics research.

This site is my public research page and my journal. The research page lists my papers, the journal holds notes on what I am reading and working through, and the projects page covers things I build at home.

Research interests

Industrial organization · Consumer consideration and choice · Economics of healthcare markets · Digital markets · Applied econometrics and machine learning · Interpretability of language models
